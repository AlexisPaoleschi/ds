The Venue

A historic mansion in the heart of Lyon

L'Hôtel Napoléon is a stunning 335m² private residence built in 1910, a masterpiece of neo-Renaissance Italian architecture. Completely renovated in 2020 while preserving its period character, this classified historic property offers an exceptional setting for artistic photography.

Neo-Renaissance architecture

Façades, art nouveau mosaics, Louis XV salon, monumental wooden staircase with Jacques Gruber stained glass

Luxurious accommodations

Seven private bedrooms, four bathrooms, period furnishings with Louis XV, Louis XVI, and Napoleon décor

Exclusive shooting locations

Heated saltwater pool with spa bench, 1650m² landscaped park, fountain, multiple shooting locations throughout the estate

The venue is conveniently located with easy access by car

Frequently Asked Questions

What level of photography experience do I need?

Basic photography skills are required. You should be comfortable operating your camera in manual mode and understand fundamental concepts like exposure, aperture, and shutter speed. This workshop is designed for photographers who want to refine their artistic nude photography skills.

What equipment should I bring?

You'll need your own camera and at least one lens suitable for portrait photography. A 50mm lens is recommended as a minimum. Don't forget extra memory cards, batteries, and any other equipment you typically use for portrait work.

What are the payment terms?

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to book your spot. You can choose to pay the full amount upfront, or pay the remaining 50% by May 8, 2026 (one month before the workshop begins).

What is the cancellation policy?

The 50% deposit is non-refundable. If you need to cancel, the deposit cannot be refunded. If the organizer cancels the workshop, you will receive a full refund. In the unlikely event that a model is unavailable, the organizer will provide an equivalent replacement model.

What language will the workshop be conducted in?

The workshop will be conducted in English and French. All instruction, communication, and group discussions will be in both languages.

How many photographers will attend?

The workshop is limited to 6 photographers maximum. This intimate group size ensures you'll have ample shooting time with each model, working in pairs (2 photographers per model) throughout the three shooting days.

Are meals and accommodation included?

Yes, absolutely! Your investment includes a private luxury bedroom at l'Hôtel Napoléon, all meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), and unlimited hot and cold beverages throughout your stay.

Is transport from the station or airport included?

Yes, transport from Lyon Part-Dieu train station, Lyon Perrache train station, or Lyon Saint-Exupéry airport to the venue is included in the workshop price. We'll coordinate pickup times with you before your arrival. If you're driving, free parking is available on-site at the mansion.

Can I use the photos I take for my portfolio?

Yes, you retain full copyright of all images you create. A model release agreement is signed with each model before the event, covering permitted uses. You may also voluntarily share images with the model for their own self-promotion. Commercial use always requires a separate agreement.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Please inform us of any dietary restrictions or allergies when booking. We'll ensure all meals accommodate your needs.
