Frequently Asked Questions

What level of photography experience do I need?

Basic photography skills are required. You should be comfortable operating your camera in manual mode and understand fundamental concepts like exposure, aperture, and shutter speed. This workshop is designed for photographers who want to refine their artistic nude photography skills.

What equipment should I bring?

You'll need your own camera and at least one lens suitable for portrait photography. A 50mm lens is recommended as a minimum. Don't forget extra memory cards, batteries, and any other equipment you typically use for portrait work.
